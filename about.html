About Laura's Sweet Delight


Our History

Laura's Sweet Delight was founded in 2021 by a passionate young South African lady who is a true candy enthusiast. The aim of the business is to bring joy through colorful, nostalgic sweets. Originally starting out with manual ordering over WhatsApp, this platform expanded to offer customers an automated, easy online shopping experience.

Our Mission

To spread happiness by offering high-quality, fun, and affordable candies to families, children, and candy lovers across South Africa.

Our Vision

To become the leading online candy destination in South Africa and beyond.
